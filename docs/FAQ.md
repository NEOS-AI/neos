## Where's the name NEOS come from?

NEOS is short for **D**eep **E**xploration and **E**fficient **R**esearch **Flow**. It is named after the deer, which is a symbol of gentleness and elegance. We hope NEOS can bring a gentle and elegant deep research experience to you.
